Medical Biography

Hands-on clinical mastery. Zero compromises.

Dr. Devansh Sharma founded DS Polyclinic with a resolute medical principle: high-grade physical therapy must be delivered directly by a consultant, not passed off to rotating junior assistants or replaced with unattended heat packs.

Holding both a Bachelor’s and Master’s Degree in Orthopaedic Physiotherapy (BPT, MPT), Dr. Sharma has spent more than 10 years treating complex degenerative spine issues, acute athletic injuries, and challenging post-surgical joint replacements. His clinical intuition is grounded in meticulous functional biomechanics and kinetic chain assessment.

"Our objective is not temporary pain masking. It is restoring the anatomical integrity of movement so you can return to life, sport, and work without fear."

Practice Standards

Four pillars of every treatment plan.

How our consultations differ from typical commercial therapy chains.

01

Diagnostic Thoroughness

We allocate 45 to 60 minutes for every initial evaluation. We examine your spine, posture, joint mobility, muscle firing sequences, and radiological imaging before recommending any protocol.

02

Evidence-Based Modalities

We utilize scientifically validated manual techniques (Maitland, Mulligan, McKenzie) combined with precision electrotherapy to promote true cellular tissue regeneration.

03

Conservative Surgery Prevention

A significant percentage of disc bulges, frozen shoulders, and knee arthritis cases can be fully resolved conservatively with structured physical therapy, eliminating the need for surgery.
