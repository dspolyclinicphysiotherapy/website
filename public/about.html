The Doctor.

Consultant-led care. Every session, every patient, personally.

Dr. Devansh Sharma

Consultant Physiotherapist

Dr. Devansh Sharma practises at DS Polyclinic in Noida Extension. He provides one-to-one, consultant-delivered sessions — every patient is assessed and treated personally, never delegated to an assistant.

His approach combines hands-on manual therapy with structured rehabilitation protocols tailored to each patient's condition and lifestyle. No rushing, no cookie-cutter plans.

Whether it's chronic back pain, a sports injury, post-surgical recovery, or neurological rehabilitation — Dr. Sharma takes the time to understand your body, your pain, and your goals before laying a hand on you.

Approach

How treatment works here.

01

Listen & assess

A thorough one-to-one movement assessment. No clipboard questionnaires — Dr. Sharma watches you move, tests your joints, and finds the root cause.

02

Treat hands-on

Manual therapy, mobilisation, and targeted interventions applied directly. Every minute of your session is consultant-delivered treatment time.

03

Build a plan

A structured home exercise programme and follow-up schedule tailored to your body, your pain, and your daily life. Clear milestones, not vague timelines.
