Our Story & Doctors

Built on Empathy, Clinical Science & Real Hands-On Healing

We founded AuraSpine with a straightforward pledge: treat every patient as family, spend full 1-on-1 time, and eliminate physical pain at its root cause without unnecessary surgery.

A Note from Our Founder: Why We Do Physiotherapy Differently

"Over my 14 years of hospital and clinical practice in Bengaluru, I noticed a troubling pattern in modern physiotherapy centers: patients would arrive in acute agony, get strapped to a passive electrotherapy machine for 15 minutes, and then be sent home with a generic photocopy of exercises without any real physical therapist actually touching their spine or testing their movement.

That is not real physiotherapy. Real recovery requires skilled clinical hands to mobilize stiff spinal joints, deactivate deep muscular trigger points with dry needling, and restore biomechanical movement.

At AuraSpine, we operate on a strict 1-on-1 private care model. When you book a slot, that time belongs 100% to you and your senior physiotherapist. That is how we have helped over 3,500+ patients regain pain-free mobility across Bengaluru."

👨‍⚕️
Dr. Aakash Sharma, BPT, MPT, MIAP
Chief Clinical Director, AuraSpine Physiotherapy
