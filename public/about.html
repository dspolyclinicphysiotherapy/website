Our Approach

The Clinical Standards Behind Every Session

Standard 01 · Assessment

Understand before treating

Every case opens with a full movement screen — posture, range, strength and the daily habits that keep a problem alive. Nothing is treated before it is understood.

Standard 02 · Treatment

Hands-on and progressive

Manual therapy to restore joint play, targeted exercise to rebuild control, adjusted at every session to how your tissue responds.

Standard 03 · The Goal

Built so you can leave

A short home programme, re-tests and a clear discharge point. Success is measured by no longer needing us.

Need a Bedside Home Visit?

For elderly patients, post-operative joint surgery cases, or anyone for whom traveling to SS Plaza is the obstacle — Dr. Devansh delivers the exact same clinical protocol at your bedside across Noida Extension and Greater Noida.
