How It Works

Four steps. One plan.

No open-ended treatment packages. You are re-tested at every stage so progress is measured, not assumed.

STEP 01

Assess

A full movement screen — posture, range, strength and the history behind the pain. Nothing is treated before it is understood.

STEP 02

Diagnose

We isolate what is driving the problem and, more importantly, what keeps re-triggering it in your day.

STEP 03

Treat

Manual therapy, modalities and graded exercise — adjusted at every session to how the tissue responds.

STEP 04

Rebuild

A short home programme, re-tests and a clear discharge point. The goal is for you to no longer need us.
