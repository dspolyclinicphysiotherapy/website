Consultant Philosophy

How clinical care is delivered here.

We do not believe in 10-minute machine attachments or unsupervised exercises. Every patient receives rigorous clinical attention.

01

100% Consultant Delivered

Your treatment is never delegated to an inexperienced assistant or intern. Dr. Devansh Sharma examines, adjusts, and treats you directly in every session.

02

Root Cause Resolution

We investigate kinetic chain dysfunction, muscle imbalances, and joint restrictions rather than merely numbing local symptoms with temporary modalities.

03

Objective Progression

Every phase has quantifiable benchmarks: range of motion in degrees, pain reduction scores, and functional mobility tests so you see measurable weekly milestones.

Therapy 05

Cupping & Deep Fascial Release

Certified clinical dry cupping, wet cupping (Hijama), and moving myofascial vacuum therapy. Negative pressure enhances local microcirculation and releases rigid fibrous fascia.

  • Negative pressure decompression for stubborn muscular knots
  • Accelerated cellular metabolic exchange and toxic byproduct drainage
  • Safe, medical-grade single-use sterile apparatus
  • Highly effective for upper back tightness, trapezius spasms, and sciatica
